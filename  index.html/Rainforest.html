Welcome to Rainforest Falls VBS, where adventure meets faith! Step into a vibrant rainforest filled with exciting activities, powerful Bible stories, and unforgettable moments. Just like the waterfalls that never stop flowing, God’s love for us is endless and refreshing.


🌴At Rainforest Falls VBS, children will explore the beauty of God’s creation while learning important life lessons from the Bible. Through games, music, crafts, and storytelling, we help kids grow stronger in faith and build lasting friendships.Just like a waterfall flows endlessly, God’s love never runs dry. In every challenge and every joy, He is always with us—guiding, protecting, and helping us grow.🌊
